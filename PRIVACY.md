# Privacy Policy

*Notice: This document is an initial draft describing the data practices of the tools in this repository. It should be reviewed by legal counsel before commercial launch.*

Last updated: 2026-11-09

## 1. Overview

The tools and browser extensions in this repository are designed with privacy in mind. They operate locally on your machine and in your browser.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

The tools in this repository do not collect, transmit, or share:

- Personal identification information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers).
- Browsing histories, page URLs, or web page content.
- AI chat contents, prompts, or responses.
- Telemetry, usage statistics, or analytics data.

All data inspection and action execution performed by extensions (such as TabPilot and AI Chat Exporter) occur strictly inside your local browser memory and between the browser tabs you designate.

## 3. Local Storage

Extensions in this repository may use browser storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.session`) solely to store user preferences on your device, such as:

- Selected target tab identifier.
- Configured step execution delay and compact mode toggles.
- Recent in-memory action logs for debugging.

This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time through the extension settings or by removing the extension.

## 4. Third-Party Web Services

When you use extensions with third-party web platforms (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Meta), your interaction with those platforms is governed solely by the respective privacy policies and terms of those providers.
